# Worker Contract

Status: `draft_round_001`

Worker responsibilities:

- respond to reviewer findings explicitly
- revise design artifacts without erasing prior evidence
- preserve redirect and archive traceability

Worker must produce:

- updated draft output
- per-question response notes
- unresolved-risk notes when a blocker cannot yet be closed
- and, when needed, explicit:
  - `Strategic Challenge`
  - `Rule Objection`
  - `Evidence-Based Shortcut`

Worker may not:

- collapse archive and canonical layers
- claim closure without reviewer-facing evidence
- stay silent when the local task framing is obviously inferior to the evidence-backed path
