# Flat Communication Protocol

Status: `draft_round_003`

## Core Rule

Workflow communication should be as flat as possible.

## Meaning

- reviewer may challenge worker output directly
- explorer may challenge worker assumptions directly
- worker may challenge reviewer expectations directly
- subagents may surface strategic objections directly through structured filesystem artifacts

## Constraint

Direct communication does not mean informal communication.

All flat communication must still be:

- structured
- file-backed
- attributable
- reviewable

## Goal

Prevent needless hierarchy loops where valid first-principles objections get delayed or diluted.
